Passivhaus is a standard, but the ideas underneath it are simple: insulate well, stop draughts, avoid cold bridges, and bring in fresh air without throwing the heat away.

You do not need to certify an extension to benefit. Most of the gain is in the first three.

## Where to start

1. Find out where the heat goes. A thermal survey costs less than a radiator.
2. Fix the airtightness of what you are keeping before you add anything new.
3. Design the new part to the full standard, even if the old part never will be.
