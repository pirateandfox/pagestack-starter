When we bought 2 Harbour Street in 2021 it was a net store: one tall room, granite walls a metre thick, a slate roof held up by habit.

## What we kept

Almost everything you can see from the street. The walls, the big doors onto the quay, the roof line. Planning would have asked us to anyway, but we would have kept them regardless; the building's shape is why it is good to work in.

## What we changed

We put a timber frame inside the old walls, so the new floor and roof stand on their own and the granite only has to keep the weather out. The long table sits under a new roof light, on the north side, so the light on the drawings is even all day.

> Old buildings are generous. They give you thickness, and thickness is what makes a room calm.

## What we would do differently

Insulate the floor before anything else. We left it to last and spent a winter in coats.
