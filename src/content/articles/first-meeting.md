People often apologise for not having a plan. You don't need one; that is our job.

What helps most:

- **Three things you love** about where you live now.
- **Three things that drive you mad.**
- **A budget**, even a rough one. It is the most useful number in the room.
- **Photos** of buildings you like, from anywhere.
